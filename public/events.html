Latch-Up in Waterloo

In May 2026, members of Schulich on a Chip travelled to the University of Waterloo for Latch-Up 2026, hosted by the FOSSi Foundation. Our team learned from leaders in open-source silicon, toured the Quantum-Nano Fabrication Centre, participated in a Tiny Tapeout chip-design workshop, and delivered a lightning talk sharing our mission, first-year progress, and plans for the future.
